# The 2026 State of the League Address
*As prepared for delivery by the Commissioner-for-a-Night, Ian ("The Human Coin-Flip")*

---

My fellow managers. Distinguished champions, decorated runners-up, and Peter.

We gather, as we do each year, in a spirit of fellowship, mutual respect, and the shared
understanding that all of you draft like the money isn't real. Before the gavel falls and
the auction begins, the record demands a full and honest accounting. The State of our
League is **overleveraged, overconfident, and — as ever — bidding $50 on a backup running
back.**

Let the data speak, for it does not blink.

**On the matter of fiscal discipline:** this body has established, across three audited
seasons, that it will pay roughly **fair value for a stud, a small discount for a solid
starter, and two-and-a-half times fair value for a $1 dart throw.** You do not have a
process. You have a gambling problem with a projection tab open. The league is
statistically broke by Week 5, at which point the waiver wire — where the actual
championships are decided — is worked by the desperate and the disciplined alike.

**Honors and censures from the wire**, entered into the permanent record:

The Medal of Fiscal Genius is awarded to **Derek**, who spent seventy-five dollars on Puka
Nacua and was, for once, correct. Commendations to **Alec**, whose two-dollar Sam Darnold
outscored most of your first-round picks, and to your Commissioner, who quietly rostered a
three-dollar Trevor Lawrence for three hundred and thirty-eight points and told no one.

The censures are heavier. Let the chamber note **Zach's** ninety-dollar Isaac Guerendo — the
largest bid in recorded history, exchanged for the fantasy output of a damp paper towel — a
sum matched in spirit only by his twenty-four-dollar Zach Evans, who returned **one point
and ninety cents.** **Alec's** fifty-dollar Carson Steele and **Gerk's** forty-one-dollar
Emari Demercado are hereby entered as cautionary tales for future generations.

**The roll of the realm**, that all may be seen and none may hide:

- **Gerk, the Cursed King** — four rings, and the unluckiest man ever to play this game. The schedule owes you money.
- **Simon, the Metronome** — reigning champion, best average finish in the league. We are aware. You needn't keep proving it.
- **Derek, the Regular-Season Tyrant** — the finest record we've ever produced, and two titles in five trips to the final. September belongs to you. January does not.
- **Ryan, Always the Bridesmaid** — one ring, five second-place finishes. At this point the runner-up trophy should be renamed.
- **Dawkins, the Avalanche** — architect of the 224-point beatdown that ended the 2024 final before halftime. A war crime, beautifully executed.
- **Zach, the Horseshoe** — the luckiest manager alive, with the receipts to prove neither skill nor restraint was involved.
- **Andy, the Basement Baron** — the worst record among us, and yet: a ring, and last year's final. Chaos incarnate.
- **Joles, the Waiver Hawk** — sixty-five claims, more than any human should file. Touch grass.
- **Chance, the High Roller** — shops least, bids biggest. A man of few transactions and enormous, occasionally regrettable, conviction.
- **Alec, the Overbidder** — the highest average bid in the league. Both the best and the worst pickup in this speech belong to you. That is who you are.
- **Peter, the Ringless Wonder** — fourteen seasons. Zero titles. Zero appearances in a title game. A streak of such purity it deserves its own banner.

And your Commissioner? A career .500 coin-flip with a single 2017 banner and a 15-and-4
record against Chance that I will be mentioning again. I make no promises tonight. History
suggests I am exactly as likely to win this draft as to lose it.

The auction is now open. The projections are printed, the budgets are set, and by the third
round every plan in this room will be on fire. Bid well. Bid poorly. Just bid quickly.

**Good luck. You'll need more of it than Gerk.**
