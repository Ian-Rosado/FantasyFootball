# Gridiron Grind — Manager Profiles & the FAAB Hall of Fame/Shame

Source material for the draft-night opening statement. All records are all-time; FAAB
grades cover the ESPN era (2023–25) using full-season, league-scored actual points
(2021–22 lived on Fleaflicker and isn't point-graded). Full-season totals slightly flatter
players who produced before being added, and QBs top the raw points-per-dollar lists
because they score most and start every week — read the steals with that in mind.

## FAAB Hall of Fame (best pickups, 2023–25)

**Dollar-store MVPs** (cheap adds, full-season points):
- **Sam Darnold — $2 → 308 pts** (Alec, 2024). The best value pickup in the data. A $2 QB1.
- **Sam LaPorta — $2 → 239** (Gerk, 2023). Elite TE off the scrap heap.
- **Trevor Lawrence — $3 → 338** (Ian, 2025). Ian's own masterpiece — top-scoring QB for pocket change.
- **C.J. Stroud — $2 → 220** (Simon, 2024) · **Rico Dowdle — $3 → 216** (Gerk, 2025) · **Quentin Johnston — $2 → 175** (Dawkins, 2024).

**Best big-money bid ever:** **Puka Nacua — $75 → 298 pts** (Derek, 2023). Paid up huge and landed a league-winner. The one time overpaying was genius.

## FAAB Hall of Shame (busts & boldest gambles)

**Priciest busts** (paid up, got nothing):
- **Carson Steele — $50 → 24 pts** (Alec, 2024). The worst big splurge in the data.
- **Emari Demercado — $41 → 73** (Gerk, 2023).
- **Hassan Haskins — $27 → 10** (Simon, 2025) · **Devin Neal — $27 → 60** (Joles, 2025) · **Keaton Mitchell — $26 → 70** (Joles, 2023).
- **Zach Evans — $24 → 1.9 pts** (Zach, 2023). Twenty-four dollars for basically a single good afternoon.
- **Tank Bigsby — $22 → 54** (Peter, 2025).

**Boldest bids in league history** (bid → what they got):
- **$90 Isaac Guerendo → 94** (Zach, 2024) — the biggest bid ever, for a backup's worth of points.
- **$85 Dontayvion Wicks → 111** (Derek, 2024) · **$77 Emanuel Wilson → 95** (Gerk, 2025).
- **$55 Jauan Jennings → 211** (Andy, 2024) — a rare big-bid hit.
- **$51 Kayshon Boutte → 124** (Chance, 2025) · **$50 Carson Steele → 24** (Alec, 2024) — same week's lesson in variance.

## Quick angles for the statement
- The league **overpays $1 sleepers ~2.6× fair value** and **blows its FAAB by Week 5** — yet the best pickups were all $2–3. The wire rewards patience, not panic.
- Everyone has a skeleton: Zach's $90 whiff, Alec's $50 Steele, Gerk's $41 Demercado, Peter's $22 Bigsby, Simon's $27 Haskins.
- And a trophy: Derek's $75 Puka, Alec's $2 Darnold, Gerk's $2 LaPorta, Ian's $3 Lawrence.
